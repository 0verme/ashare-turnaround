## Correctness-only findings

- Target selection and feature/PIT cutoffs are separate: `trade_cal` is read
  unprojected for target selection, while each target's feature frames remain
  bounded at its selected trading date (`2025-06` remains `20250616`).
- `ru_maxrss` is retained as `peak_rss_diagnostic_bytes`; live PSS/private,
  current process swap, MemAvailable, and system swap growth are the hard
  resource metrics under `resource-gate-v2`.

- Financial visibility is bounded by `actual_available_date <= as_of`, including
  the original/revised version boundary fixture.
- Market and benchmark evidence is bounded by the selected trading session.
- Missing benchmark evidence stays `UNKNOWN`; no stock-only fallback is used.
- Current `stock_basic` name/status/industry/board fields are
  `UNSUPPORTED_PIT` and are never backfilled into historical rows.
- Every candidate retains feature evidence, score partialness, coverage,
  confidence, unknown groups, ranking eligibility, rejection reasons, and
  provenance in the per-snapshot artifact.
- The fixed manual-review artifact contains Top-3, high-score ineligible,
  unknown-heavy, and universe-exclusion cases when available.
